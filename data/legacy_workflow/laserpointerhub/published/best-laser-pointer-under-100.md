## Blue vs Green Laser: Which Is More Visible?

This is the most misunderstood part.

### Reality:
- Green lasers (532nm) appear **much brighter**
- Blue lasers (450nm) are **more powerful but less visible**

According to:
- https://www.lasersafetyfacts.com/hazard_distance_chart.html
- https://www.astroleague.org/files/u220/Astro%20Note%20A2%20-%20Laser%20Pointers.pdf

Human eyes are most sensitive to green light. That means:

- Green lasers can appear **10–30× brighter**
- Blue lasers feel stronger, but don’t look brighter

---

### What Should You Choose?

**Choose GREEN if:**
- You want maximum visibility in the night sky
- You are doing stargazing or constellation pointing

**Choose BLUE if:**
- You want higher power for the price
- You care about beam intensity and focus
- You prefer stable diode-based systems

## What Most Buyers Get Wrong About “High Power”

Power alone doesn’t define performance.

What actually matters:

### 1. Beam Quality
A tight beam travels farther and looks clearer.

### 2. Divergence
Lower divergence = longer visible distance

### 3. Stability
Cheap lasers lose power quickly

### 4. Thermal Design
Good cooling = consistent performance

## High Power Laser Safety (Critical)

According to the FAA:
https://www.faa.gov/about/initiatives/lasers

- Pointing lasers at aircraft is illegal
- Thousands of incidents happen every year

---

### Eye Safety Basics

From:
https://www.laserpointersafety.com/laser-hazard-distances-chart.html

Risk depends on:
- Power
- Distance
- Exposure time

Even brief exposure can cause:
- Flash blindness  
- Permanent eye damage  

---

### Basic Safety Rules

- Never point at people or vehicles  
- Never aim at aircraft  
- Use protective goggles  
- Avoid reflective surfaces
