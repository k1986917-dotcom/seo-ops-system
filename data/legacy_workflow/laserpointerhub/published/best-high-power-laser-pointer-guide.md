## Can High Power Lasers Burn Materials?

Under controlled conditions, high power lasers can interact with materials like dark paper or plastic.

Factors that affect burning:

- Focus precision (tighter focus = more concentrated energy)  
- Surface color (darker colors absorb more easily)  
- Distance (shorter distance = more effective)  

⚠ Note: This is only a description of physical phenomena. Always use lasers safely.We advise users not to conduct such tests, as doing so could result in fire or eye damage.
